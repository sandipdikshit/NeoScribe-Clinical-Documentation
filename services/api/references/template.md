**SOAP Note**

**Subjective:**  
The patient reports experiencing pain in the upper right shoulder, which has persisted for one week. The pain was noted upon leaving the examination room.

**Objective:**  
Upon examination, the pain is localized to the upper right shoulder, suggesting a probable tendon-related issue. No signs of swelling or bruising are observed. The patient is able to perform limited range of motion, indicating possible discomfort but no significant loss of function.

**Assessment:**  
The patient's symptoms and physical examination findings are consistent with a tendon-related injury in the upper right shoulder. Differential diagnoses could include tendonitis or impingement syndrome. 

**Plan:**  
1. Prescribe naproxen 500 mg to be taken twice a day for pain and inflammation.
2. Schedule an ultrasound to further evaluate the shoulder tendons and assess for potential tears or other injuries.
3. Educate the patient on rest and the importance of avoiding activities that exacerbate the pain.
4. Schedule a follow-up appointment in two weeks to reassess symptoms and discuss ultrasound results.
